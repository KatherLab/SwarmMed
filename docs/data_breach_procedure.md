# Data Breach Notification Procedure

**Date:** January 2, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  

This document outlines the procedure to be followed by MediSwarmCloud Organization in the event of a personal data breach, in compliance with GDPR Articles 33 and 34.

## 1. Definition of a Personal Data Breach
A personal data breach is a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, personal data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed.

## 2. Step 1: Identification and Reporting
Any employee or contractor who suspects a data breach has occurred must immediately report it to:
- **Security Lead:** security-alerts@swarmcloud.example.com
- **DPO:** dpo@swarmcloud.example.com

## 3. Step 2: Investigation and Containment
The Security Team will:
1.  **Validate:** Confirm if a breach has indeed occurred.
2.  **Contain:** Take immediate steps to stop the breach (e.g., isolate compromised servers, rotate credentials, block malicious IP addresses).
3.  **Assess:** Determine the scope and nature of the breach, including the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records involved.

## 4. Step 3: Risk Assessment
The DPO and Security Lead will assess the risk to the rights and freedoms of the affected individuals.
- **Low Risk:** Unlikely to result in a risk to individuals (e.g., data was encrypted and keys were not compromised).
- **High Risk:** Likely to result in a high risk to individuals (e.g., sensitive identity data or PHI metadata leaked).

## 5. Step 4: Notification to Supervisory Authority (Article 33)
If the breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals, the DPO must notify the relevant Supervisory Authority within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach.
- **Supervisory Authority:** [Contact details of the lead authority]
- **Required Information:**
    - Nature of the breach.
    - Name and contact details of the DPO.
    - Likely consequences of the breach.
    - Measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

## 6. Step 5: Notification to Affected Individuals (Article 34)
If the breach is likely to result in a **high risk** to the rights and freedoms of individuals, MediSwarmCloud will notify the affected individuals without undue delay.
- Notification will be sent via the registered email address.
- It will describe the nature of the breach in clear and plain language and provide recommendations for the individuals to mitigate potential adverse effects.

## 7. Step 6: Documentation and Review
All breaches, including those that do not require notification, must be documented in the Internal Data Breach Register. A post-mortem analysis will be conducted to prevent future occurrences.
