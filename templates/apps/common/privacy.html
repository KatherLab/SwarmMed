3. Data We Collect

As a decentralized platform, we prioritize data minimization. We collect:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, and hashed passwords.
  • Profile Information: Optional information you provide such as your full name, country, and organization.
  • Usage Data: Logs of system interactions (login times, project creation).
  • Technical Data: IP addresses and browser information are collected for security and auditing purposes.

Crucially, MedSwarmHub does not centrally collect or aggregate your raw medical datasets. While the platform provides secure, project-specific storage for your datasets to facilitate local validation and swarm training, this data remains within your isolated project environment and is never shared across institutions or used for purposes beyond your specified research goals.

4. Legal Basis and Purposes

We process your data based on the following legal grounds (GDPR Article 6):

  • Performance of a Contract: To provide and maintain our services, manage your account, and provide customer support.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): To ensure system security, prevent fraud, and maintain platform integrity. This includes the logging of IP addresses to detect and mitigate unauthorized access or malicious activities.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with legal requirements such as tax laws or law enforcement requests.
  • Consent: For non-essential cookies where applicable.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your profile information at any time via your account settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can delete your account and all associated personal data through your profile settings. Audit logs will be anonymized to remove identifiers.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing: You have the right to request that we suspend the processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Object: You can object to the processing of your personal data where we are relying on a legitimate interest.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can download a machine-readable export of your account data from your profile settings.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where we are relying on consent, you have the right to withdraw it at any time.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

11. Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) Summary

MedSwarmHub conducts periodic Data Protection Impact Assessments to identify and minimize the data protection risks of our project.

11.1 Nature and Necessity

The platform facilitates collaborative machine learning on medical datasets. Processing is necessary to enable privacy-preserving medical research where traditional centralization is prohibited or risky. Our "data stays local" approach is the most proportional way to achieve these goals.

11.2 Risk Assessment & Mitigation

Risk Mitigation
Unauthorized access to account data MFA, RBAC, Encryption.
Model inversion attacks Differential Privacy (DP) and Secure Aggregation.
Data breach of metadata/logs Audit log signing, TLS 1.3, managed DB security.
Malicious participant Byzantine-robust aggregation algorithms.

13. Data Breach Notification Procedure

This procedure is followed in the event of a personal data breach, in compliance with GDPR Articles 33 and 34.

  1. Identification: Any suspected breach must be immediately reported to the Security Lead and DPO.
  2. Containment & Assessment: The Security Team will validate the breach, take immediate steps to stop it, and assess the scope and nature of the affected data.
  3. Risk Assessment: The DPO and Security Lead will assess the risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals.
  4. Notification to Authority: If the breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals, the DPO will notify the relevant Supervisory Authority within 72 hours.
  5. Notification to Individuals: If the breach is likely to result in a high risk to individuals, MedSwarmHub will notify the affected individuals without undue delay.
  6. Documentation: All breaches are documented in an Internal Data Breach Register, and a post-mortem analysis is conducted.
